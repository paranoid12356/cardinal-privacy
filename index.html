Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

CARDinal ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information — Your email address when you create an account or sign in.
  • Collection Data — Pokémon cards, graded slabs, and sealed products you add to your collection, including quantities, purchase prices, and custom price overrides.
  • Watchlist Data — Cards you choose to track and any price alerts you set.
  • Camera Usage — Camera access is used solely to scan Pokémon card images for identification and grading purposes. Images are sent to our server for processing and are not stored permanently.
  • Usage Data — Basic app usage information such as search queries and feature interactions, used to improve the app experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain your card collection and portfolio tracking
  • To identify Pokémon cards via camera scanning
  • To fetch and display real-time pricing data for cards and sealed products
  • To send price alert notifications when watchlisted cards reach your target price
  • To calculate and display your portfolio value and history
  • To improve and optimize app features and performance

Third-Party Services

CARDinal uses the following third-party services to deliver its features:

  • Supabase — Secure database and authentication provider. Your account and collection data is stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Scrydex — Provides Pokémon TCG card and sealed product pricing data from TCGplayer market prices.
  • Google Gemini AI — Used to identify Pokémon cards from camera images. Card images are processed by Google's AI and are not stored by Google beyond the immediate request.
  • PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator) — Used to look up graded card certification details by certificate number.
  • eBay — Sold listing data is used to provide graded card price comparisons.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with row-level security, meaning only you can access your own collection data. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third parties.

Data Retention

We retain your account and collection data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

CARDinal is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your collection data via the in-app export feature

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
