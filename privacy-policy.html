Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

The short version: CARDinal is a trading card portfolio tracker. We collect only what we need to run the app — your email, your collection data, basic usage information, and a few things you give us when you ask the AI assistant for help. We don't sell your data. We don't show ads. We use Anthropic and Google Cloud AI to power the assistant; we use Supabase to store your data; we use Apple for in-app purchases. That's it.

1. Who We Are

CARDinal is a mobile application for tracking trading card collections — Pokémon (English and Japanese) and One Piece TCG, including raw, graded, and sealed products. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use CARDinal, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

If you have questions about anything in this policy, email us at supportcardinal@gmail.com.

2. What We Collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: email address, optional username, and any contact preferences you set.
  • Collection data: the cards you add to your CARDinal collection, including card identifiers, condition, grade, purchase price (if you provide it), and notes.
  • Photos: if you use the in-app card scanner, we process the camera image to identify the card. Card photos are not stored on our servers unless you explicitly save them to your collection.
  • AI assistant inputs: messages you send to the AI assistant. These are processed by our AI service providers (Anthropic and Google Cloud AI) to generate responses.
  • Support communications: if you email us, we keep the message and your reply address so we can respond.

Information we collect automatically

  • Device information: device model, OS version, app version, language, and a generated user identifier so we can sync your data across sessions.
  • Usage data: which screens you visit, which features you use, how often you open the app. We use this to improve CARDinal.
  • Crash and performance logs: if the app crashes or hits an error, we collect diagnostic information to fix the bug.
  • IP address: recorded when you connect to our servers, used for security and to comply with applicable law.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location data (GPS).
  • We do not collect health, fitness, or biometric data.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, or other apps.
  • We do not store your payment card information — Apple processes all in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect data from anyone we know to be under 13. CARDinal is intended for users 13+.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use what we collect for these purposes:

  • Run the app: show your collection, calculate portfolio values, fetch card prices, sync across your devices.
  • Provide the AI assistant: route your questions to our AI service providers and return their responses to you, with relevant context from your collection where applicable.
  • Improve CARDinal: understand what's working, what isn't, and where users get stuck.
  • Communicate with you: reply to support requests, send transactional emails (e.g., password reset), and — only if you opt in — occasional product updates.
  • Security and fraud prevention: detect abuse, prevent unauthorized access, and protect the service.
  • Comply with the law: respond to lawful requests from authorities and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. AI Assistant

CARDinal includes an AI assistant that answers questions about your collection and the trading card market. Here's how it works:

  • When you send a message to the assistant, we forward your message — along with relevant context from your collection (e.g., cards you own, recent price data) — to one of our AI service providers: Anthropic (anthropic.com) or Google Cloud AI (cloud.google.com).
  • These providers process your message and return a response. They do not retain your data for training their models, per their enterprise terms with us.
  • We log the conversation history within your account so you can refer back to past messages. You can delete this history at any time from within the app.
  • We do not use your AI assistant conversations for advertising or sell them to third parties.

5. Who We Share Data With

We share your data only with the service providers we need to run CARDinal. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — stores your account, collection data, and app usage logs.
  • Anthropic (anthropic.com) — processes AI assistant requests using Claude.
  • Google Cloud AI (cloud.google.com) — processes AI assistant requests using Gemini.
  • Apple (apple.com) — handles in-app purchases, subscription billing, and Sign In with Apple.
  • Card data providers — TCGPlayer, Pokémon TCG API, OPTCG API, and others we use to fetch live card prices and metadata. We send card identifiers (not your personal information) to these services.
  • Hosting and analytics infrastructure — Railway (railway.app), Cloudflare (cloudflare.com), and standard analytics tools used to keep the app running and detect issues.

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices and we share only what is necessary for them to perform their function. Where we share personal data with providers based outside your country, we rely on appropriate legal safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EEA/UK).

We may also share information if required by law (subpoena, court order, valid legal request) or to protect the rights, safety, or property of CARDinal, our users, or the public.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights regarding your personal data. These include:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate information.
  • The right to delete your account and associated data.
  • The right to export your data in a portable format.
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications.
  • The right to withdraw consent where we rely on consent to process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at supportcardinal@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

If you're in the European Economic Area, UK, or Switzerland

You have the rights described above under the GDPR (or UK GDPR / Swiss FADP). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe we've handled your data improperly.

If you're in California

You have the rights described above under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can request a copy of your data, ask us to delete it, or correct inaccuracies. You will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

If you're elsewhere in the United States

Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, and Virginia have similar rights under their respective state privacy laws. The same email address above works for all rights requests.

7. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your collection data and personal information from our active databases within 30 days. Some information may persist in backups for up to 90 days before being purged. We may retain certain information longer if required by law (for example, transaction records for tax purposes).

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Encrypted storage at rest via our cloud providers.
  • Access controls limiting who on our team can see customer data.
  • Regular security reviews and dependency updates.

No system is 100% secure. If we ever experience a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Children's Privacy

CARDinal is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at supportcardinal@gmail.com and we will delete the account and associated data.

10. Cookies and Tracking

The CARDinal mobile app does not use cookies. It does store a small amount of data on your device (account session token, cached card images, your preferences) so the app works offline and remembers your settings. You can clear this data by uninstalling the app.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers and we do not show ads in CARDinal.

11. Do Not Track

Because we don't track users for advertising purposes in the first place, "Do Not Track" browser signals do not affect how CARDinal handles your data — we don't track you either way.

12. Account Deletion

You can delete your CARDinal account at any time from within the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This will remove your collection data, AI conversation history, and personal information from our active systems within 30 days. Some information may persist in backups for up to 90 days as described in section 7.

Alternatively, email us at supportcardinal@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account" and we'll handle it for you.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as CARDinal evolves or as laws change. When we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and notify you in the app or by email. Continued use of CARDinal after changes means you accept the updated policy.
